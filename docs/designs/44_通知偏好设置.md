## Brand & Style

The design system is centered on the concept of **"Perceptual Serenity"**—a blend of Japanese minimalism and modern functionalism. It aims to transform the anxiety of a cluttered room into the psychological relief of a tidy, breathable space. The brand personality is pragmatic, encouraging, and healing, acting as a gentle "tidying companion" rather than a critical inspector.

The visual style utilizes **Modern Japanese Minimalism** with a **Tactile** twist. It features generous whitespace, soft organic shapes, and a high-degree of roundedness to feel approachable. The UI should evoke the sensation of a clean linen surface or a sun-drenched wooden floor—ordered yet warm.

**Design Principles:**
- **Visual Breathability:** Use ample margins to mirror the goal of "clear surfaces" in the physical world.
- **Action-Oriented Simplicity:** Information is tiered to prevent cognitive overload, adhering to the "3-step rule."
- **Soft Precision:** High-precision data (like AI bounding boxes) is rendered with soft edges and calming colors to reduce technical friction.

## Colors

The palette is inspired by natural elements—forest greens, soft morning skies, and warm wood tones—to create a "healing" atmosphere that counteracts the stress of clutter.

- **Primary (Forest Green):** Used for primary actions and successful states. It represents growth and order.
- **Secondary (Mint/Sage):** Used for progress bars, secondary buttons, and success highlights.
- **Tertiary (Mist/Neutral Blue):** Used for background layers and subtle UI elements like card borders.
- **Neutral (Soft Gray/Off-White):** Replaces harsh white (#FFFFFF) for page backgrounds to reduce eye strain and provide a "paper-like" feel.
- **Functional Accents:** `warm-amber` is used for "Atmosphere Suggestions" (lighting) and difficulty ratings, while `soft-blue` is used for technical AI overlays.

Avoid "Harsh White" light or high-contrast black; use deep charcoal for text to maintain a softer, more organic aesthetic.

## Typography

**Be Vietnam Pro** is selected for its contemporary, friendly, and highly legible character. It balances professional clarity with a warm, inviting tone.

- **Headlines:** Use a bold weight to create a strong hierarchy. Suggestions like "The Chair First Aid Method" should be treated as hero elements.
- **Body:** Standardized at 16px for optimal readability during physical activity (tidying). 
- **Labels:** Uppercase with slight letter spacing is used for metadata like "DIFFICULTY" or "TIME COST" to distinguish them from actionable instructions.
- **Numeric Display:** The tidiness score is the focal point of the analysis screen and should be rendered in a heavy weight to provide immediate feedback.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout follows a **Fixed-Width Column** approach on mobile to maintain focus, transitioning to a **Fluid Grid** on tablets. Inspired by the "Grid Method" (田字格) mentioned in the primitive data, the UI utilizes a clear 4-square or 2-column structure for comparing "Before/After" or "Action/Alternative" scenarios.

- **Margins:** Generous 24px side margins are used to create a sense of "uncluttered space" within the app itself.
- **Rhythm:** An 8px-based spacing system ensures vertical rhythm.
- **Information Density:** Strictly enforced "One Screen, One Goal." No more than three action steps are visible at once to prevent user overwhelm.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy drop shadows. Instead, it uses **Tonal Layering** and **Soft Ambient Occlusion** to indicate depth.

- **Surface Tiers:** Background uses `neutral`, while active cards use `paper-white`. This subtle contrast creates depth without visual noise.
- **Soft Shadows:** If a shadow is required (e.g., for a floating action button), use a very diffused, low-opacity green-tinted shadow (`rgba(45, 106, 79, 0.08)`) to maintain the "healing" aesthetic.
- **Glassmorphism:** Used sparingly for AI overlays and camera-view HUDs. A soft backdrop blur (10px-15px) allows the user to see their room while reading the AI analysis.

## Shapes

To convey "Affinity" and "Softness," the system uses a high degree of roundedness.

- **Standard Elements (Buttons/Inputs):** 0.5rem (8px) corner radius.
- **Cards & Content Blocks:** 1rem (16px) corner radius to feel like smooth, finished furniture or storage bins.
- **Visual Metaphors:** Bounding boxes for AI recognition should use 4px rounded corners rather than sharp angles to feel less "industrial" and more "integrated" with the domestic environment.

## Components

- **Buttons:** Primary buttons are pill-shaped with `primary_color`. Secondary buttons use an outline or a `tertiary` background. No sharp edges.
- **Action Cards:** Use a clean `paper-white` background with a subtle border. The header of the card should clearly state the "Must Do" action in a bold weight.
- **AI Bounding Boxes:** Use semi-transparent `soft-blue` or `healing-green` fills (15% opacity) with a 2px solid stroke. Label tags should be small and pill-shaped.
- **Progress/Difficulty Stars:** Use `warm-amber` for the stars. The shapes should be slightly rounded.
- **Video Players:** Embedded 15-second tutorials should have 16px rounded corners and a simple "Play" overlay, avoiding complex scrub bars to keep the interface minimal.
- **Chips/Tags:** Used for "Must Do," "Optional," and "Room Type." These use low-saturation versions of the brand colors with dark-charcoal text.
